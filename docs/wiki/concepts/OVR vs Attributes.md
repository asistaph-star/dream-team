# OVR vs Attributes

## Purpose

Keep the distinction between real-data OVR and upgradeable player attributes clear.

## Current Facts

OVR is the player's real current-season performance value.

Attributes are gameplay stats that can grow through star-up.

OVR should not be inflated by upgrades.

Example:

- A player can stay OVR 79 but have 300+ Rebound after star-up.
- This means the player is still rated by real NBA performance, but the user's card has developed stronger gameplay attributes.

## Rules

- OVR comes from NBA data and formula.
- Star-up does not change OVR.
- Star-up can increase attributes.
- Attributes are uncapped.

## Open Questions

- Should salary use OVR only, attributes only, or both?
- Should matchmaking consider OVR, attributes, or team power?
